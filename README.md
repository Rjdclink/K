# K — sports and weather trading

**Current entry policy (November 22, 2026; supersedes earlier allocation and entry descriptions below):** All otherwise qualified new trades may allocate **up to 100% of the current total bankroll per trade, including estimated fees**, never exceeding available cash, executable orderbook depth, and all unchanged independent verification, timing, and net-profit gates. **No new basketball trades of any kind** (NBA, WNBA, collegiate, international, or basketball combinations) are eligible. **No new player-prop trades of any kind** (YES/NO, countable, variable, cushioned, non-cushioned, combinations) are eligible. The prohibition covers automatic entry, recovery/retry, upgrades/add-ons, and manual purchases; it does **not** delete prior trades or prevent tracking, settlement or closing existing positions. Basketball score and position displays remain available. Existing percentage-tier language further down this README describes superseded policies or signal metadata, not an operative spending cap.

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi trading dashboard. The sports scanner reads linked live milestones, event markets and actual YES ask depth (resting NO bids). **Qualifying trades with no executable ask receive a maximum 30-second real-time liquidity window.** If the relevant YES or NO side remains empty, that exact contract/game/side is persistently skipped; repeated scanner instances must not restart the clock. A confirmed executable orderbook quote cancels an earlier no-ask timer at any age; a quote itself never expires after 30 seconds. Once 30 seconds of continuous absence have elapsed, empty-book polling is paused unless new market data indicates that a seller may have appeared, at which point the scanner performs a bounded live orderbook recheck. Other contracts for the same game, opposite-side asks, positions, settlement records and manually directed sells remain unaffected. The dashboard distinguishes waiting seconds and an empty-book skip from a verified executable quote. A time-eligible final independent verification is attempted and recorded regardless of missing liquidity, available cash, fees or projected profit; only a live, fresh score confirmation plus the normal risk and liquidity gates can authorize an order.

**Independent external verification for the four enabled sports:** Kalshi supplies the entry-clock authority. ESPN is checked first, retried once only if unavailable, then FOX Sports only if both ESPN attempts are unavailable. The external source must identify the exact two teams and verify their observed scores, with any available quarter/period scoring splits used for the selected contract. **No external clock equality is required.** An independently confirmed contradiction, ambiguous fixture, inadequate period-specific attribution, missing executable seller or insufficient cushion still blocks entry. All scoreboard date parameters use eight digits (`YYYYMMDD`); a healthy ESPN server with no matched fixture is reported as **no verified fixture**, not as a service outage. Full-game soccer NO at 3–3 has six scored goals: Over 7.5 has a **1.5-goal** cushion, Over 8.5 has **2.5 goals**, while Over 6.5 does not qualify. The scanner now records the actual rejection cause separately from the required cushion and never labels a qualifying line as missing cushion merely because ESPN/FOX is unavailable. All automatic draw and tie entries are disabled; historical position and settlement history are preserved.

**Universal fixture identity and relaxed abbreviations:** Across basketball, hockey, soccer and football, independent ESPN/FOX matching tolerates ordinary punctuation and accent differences, common abbreviations, short geographic names, and initials instead of requiring a new hardcoded alias for every club. New relaxed matches require *both* recognizable opponents in the correct home/away positions, a named competition, a kickoff within 15 minutes of Kalshi, an unambiguous provider event, and a uniquely identifiable, current external score before authorization. A matched external score that differs from Kalshi is authoritative; incorrect fixture identities or contradictory external readings remain vetoes. A successfully verified ESPN/FOX event ID is persisted by Kalshi game, provider, competition and kickoff; later checks use the ID to recognize a changed provider display name, but still fetch the live scoreboard and independently revalidate the fixture and source's current score. Unrelated clubs, overlapping ambiguous fixtures, a changed provider event ID, missing independent scores, and unsupported competition coverage fail closed. ESPN aggregate league metadata may not expose a human-readable league name for every event; sport and league context are considered but a league string alone is never treated as independent proof. No external clock comparison is introduced.

**Stale fixture reconciliation:** The sports scanner rechecks up to 12 old discovery milestones per pass using fresh Kalshi live data, with durable per-fixture review timestamps and bounded retry intervals. A score with a current provider update restores an older, genuinely live game to normal scanning; a newly confirmed final, closed or cancelled status removes that fixture from the active discovery cache (with a 24-hour re-review opportunity). A missing, contradictory or unavailable live reading is never grounds for deletion and is retried. Existing positions, trades, settlement records, manual sells, independent score verification, entry clocks and bankroll rules are untouched. ## Lead entry and allocation

Football full-game total No entries are restricted to Q4 strictly under 5:00, requiring a Kalshi contract line at least 20 points above the externally confirmed combined score for college football (CFB) or 15 points above it for the NFL. The previous **20-point / 1:15 / under-0:30** college first-half rule is superseded and must never compete for entry. Men’s and women’s college full-game total NO each require an Over line **at least 40 points above the combined score**, with a first independent external score verification starting at **8:00 or less remaining** in the final half (men) or fourth quarter (women), and entry **strictly under 7:00** only after at least 60 actual elapsed seconds and a fresh second independent verification. The separate college first-half 12-point and under-1:30 rules remain unchanged. NBA and WNBA period-one, period-two and period-three total-NO entries require a line **at least 12 points above the quarter-only combined point total**, a first independent check at **2:30 or less remaining in that quarter** (recoverable if missed), and a fresh final check after **60 elapsed real seconds** immediately before entry **strictly under 1:30** in that quarter. NBA full-game total NO requires **at least 45 points** above the combined live score; WNBA full-game total NO requires **at least 40 points**. International basketball contracts remain limited to independently matchable ESPN leagues (NBL, FIBA and Olympic basketball), with the existing full-game route. Unresolved NO orders postpone new order dispatch but do **not** suppress the independent first-check process for unrelated eligible contracts. Missing or contradictory evidence, unmatched quarter/half scores, market identity, seller liquidity, cash or net profitability blocks entry.

## Deployment

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

Discovery traverses Sports milestone pages for the preceding seven days and retains only basketball (NBA, WNBA, men’s and women’s college), football, hockey and soccer. All other sport families are excluded from fresh discovery, retained caches, live polling, market checks, entry orders, background watches and live dashboard previews. Games closest to active entry thresholds are checked first; on a once-per-minute catalog sweep, up to 48 lower-priority games are checked with reserved rotation slots to avoid starvation. Existing international-basketball exclusions remain. Pagination continues until future-only pages; the dashboard distinguishes catalog milestones from live games checked. Each entry still requires an exact linked open contract and its applicable rule.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. ## Dashboard reconciliation

Automatic exit fills are kept by Kalshi order ID before the per-market exit state is reused. History sums every archived partial sale and includes confirmed manual combo add-ons with their actual fills and fees. Uncertain manual orders are looked up by their client ID across paginated order history; a definitive rejection releases the pending request. The combo add-on quote uses the same 35% verified-lead or 15% fallback/over ceiling as the automatic combo engine.

**Calibrated settled-position profit:** Kalshi's settled and historical market-position `realized_pnl_dollars` is gross of its separately reported `fees_paid_dollars`. The account History service verifies the net subtraction against at least 25 independently reconciled fill-and-settlement markets (within one cent for each) before publishing this source; if calibration fails, existing History results remain unchanged. Successful publication uses a completion-version gate, preventing partial page batches or failed updates from changing dashboard results. The authoritative settled-position record takes precedence over the fill-only reconstruction and bot-only fallback for the same ticker, preventing duplicate profits while covering YES/NO-netted positions. No orders, risk allocations, trading decisions, or historical evidence are modified.\n\n**Position-ledger audit for netted fills:** The History worker additionally reads settled and archived Kalshi market-position records (including documented realized P&L and fees) using durable, bounded, read-only pagination. These figures are retained separately for verification against actual fills; they cannot replace History profits until exchange fee and netting treatment is validated. Trading conditions, open positions, payout calculations, historical fills and settlement records remain unaffected.\n\n**Account-wide trade history:** The History page now imports authenticated Kalshi portfolio fills, archived fills and settlements with durable cursor pagination and unique fill IDs. This includes positions purchased directly on Kalshi as well as orders placed by the bot; realized account results use actual YES/NO execution prices, filled quantities, exchange fees, sale proceeds and authoritative settlement revenue in cents. A single account-level result per ticker supersedes—not adds to—any bot-only history row for that ticker, preventing double counting. Trades without complete external evidence remain pending; the page explicitly shows when the full import is incomplete. Existing bot history remains visible for markets that cannot yet be reconciled. No historical account trades, positions or settlement records are deleted, and this feature never submits orders or changes entry rules.\n\nHistory recognizes both recorded automatic exits and manual sales by exact exchange order ID, including canonical NO-side buy representations of a YES sale. Modern fixed-point execution costs and fees are supported. Completed history is reconciled again when a recorded fill or exit changes; unavailable or incomplete fills never create an estimated result. The first history page refreshes every 30 seconds while older pages remain available.

Dashboard balance, holdings and sell requests have interactive API priority within the existing shared rate limit. They bypass queued local scanner balance refreshes. Balance cache invalidation preserves the last verified snapshot and its timestamp while preventing its reuse for new orders. The displayed bankroll remains Kalshi available cash plus Kalshi portfolio value across all exchange indexes. Sell quotes refresh in batches covering every displayed holding; errors are shown as retryable quote failures rather than being silently omitted or treated as absent bids.

Independent verification preserves the provider, exact fixture, both opponents, home/away assignment, scheduled time and known sport-specific team aliases.
