# K — sports and weather trading

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi trading dashboard. The sports scanner reads linked live milestones, event markets and actual YES ask depth (resting NO bids). It revisits score matches when an ask appears. A score match without an executable ask is displayed but cannot fill.

- **Exceeded overs:** A non-player over may enter once a recent, internally consistent Kalshi score has exceeded the exact contract line and a current seller is available. External verification and a second-score dwell are not required. An uncushioned total retains its ordinary 10% allocation; a conservative one-play cushion can support the existing 40% whole-game cap. Player-prop overs retain mandatory independent statistic verification, including cushioned player props. Cash, fees, net profit and exact contract matching remain entry gates.
- **Revised winner entries:** CFB +24 strictly under 7:30, +17 strictly under 5:00, or +12 strictly under 4:30 in Q4; the earlier tiers use the same Kalshi entry verification; NFL +11 strictly under 6:00 or +10 strictly under 2:00 in Q4; NBA +18 strictly under 7:30, +12 strictly under 6:30, or +8 strictly under 1:30 in Q4; WNBA +11 strictly under 6:00 or +7 strictly under 1:30 in Q4; men’s and women’s college basketball +16 strictly under 5:00, +10 strictly under 4:30, or +8 strictly under 2:30 in the final period (men: second half; women: Q4); international basketball uses those same three lead/time thresholds in Q4. College and international basketball do not require possession; hockey +4 strictly under 2:00 in period 3; baseball +5 at the bottom of the eighth with two outs or +4 in the ninth after at least one out; soccer +3 goals from 50′ elapsed (five minutes into the second half), or +2 goals strictly under 7:30 on the elapsed regulation clock (using the live minute when a countdown is unavailable). Rugby +15 in the second half strictly under 3:00 without the former higher allocation tier; limited-over cricket +30 runs with at most nine balls and two wickets left for the chase; golf +4 strokes in the final round with every golfer through at least 17 holes and one through exactly 17. Tennis and the unrevised handball, lacrosse, volleyball, table-tennis, darts and draw rules remain. Superseded early basketball, football and baseball winner routes and last-seconds exceptions are removed. Live quotes, exact contract identity, score confirmation, independent verification or supported conservative fallback, cash, and net profit still gate every purchase.
- **Current-day weather (paused):** When enabled, the scanner discovers rain and temperature series across cities and checks the designated station and city's local date. Entry is available throughout the named local calendar day until the earlier of market close and its local-day cutoff. The same Kalshi contract/rules/status are checked twice at least three seconds apart; an in-period matching official station observation must establish an irreversible selected outcome. No forecast-confidence percentage is required. A forecast alone cannot confirm an observed outcome. Missing or inapplicable external station data and ranges that can still change fail closed. An NWS climate measurement day can extend into the next local day during daylight saving time, but entry still stops on the named local day. Weather's measurement window is separate from sports game clocks.
- **Combinations:** Each new confirmed single fill immediately starts a fresh check against current account positions and the confirmed-fill ledger, including fills from the fast watcher, recovered orders, weather singles, and single-position upgrades. The checker considers every eligible held single, from pairs up to the collection's permitted size, when a Kalshi all-YES collection supports the exact legs and a maker returns an executable RFQ quote. An all-winner combination with independently verified legs is capped at 20% of bankroll including fees; any fallback winner leg or over leg leaves its combination capped at 10%. An unverified fallback leg may appear in at most one active or filled combination. A later independent verification can add to an existing combination position, with current position exposure and fees limiting total allocation to 10%, without selling its original fill. Immediate plans must include the newly filled leg. Combo checking runs outside the live single-order path; its work is awaited before the invocation ends. A newer fill interrupts an older unaccepted RFQ wait and starts a fresh check, while duplicate fill observations share the existing claim. Existing combo fills do not recursively create new combo checks. Rejected combinations and exact exchange errors are retained privately for the scanner; redundant thresholds and previously exchange-decided legs are excluded before new RFQs. Weather trades are independent orders when weather scanning is enabled; scheduled weather discovery and entries are currently paused to reserve resources for live sports.

Commanding-lead entries use one internally consistent Kalshi score recorded within 30 seconds, then a current seller on the exact qualifying contract. External scoreboards are optional and never hold up a new non-player entry. NBA +18 under 7:30 and +12 under 6:30 retain their existing 5%–15% sizing; other Kalshi-only leads cap at 10%, and ties at 5%. Non-player overs can use one recent Kalshi observation; player-prop overs still require an external statistic match. A failed or missing seller check cannot produce an order. Existing cash, fee, profit, duplicate-order and fill-or-kill controls apply. Independent checks remain useful for optional position upgrades and diagnostics.

On later scans, a held Kalshi-only over is independently checked against the same live contract and two Kalshi observations. When independently confirmed, it may receive one additional fill-or-kill buy on the same ticker, limited by current position exposure plus paid fees so the combined single never exceeds 10% of current bankroll including fees, or 40% when an independently recorded scoring play can be removed and the over remains crossed, including one extra credited player statistic. The original fill remains intact. A separate upgrade order and verification record preserve its history and prevent an uncertain response from creating a second order. A later independent check can also make a held fallback lead eligible for the ordinary combination schedule; it does not raise that lead's single-order 10% fallback limit.

## Lead entry and allocation

The revised winner windows are listed above. Qualification uses the score, period, clock and exact selected outcome. Entry verification checks current executable sell depth; no external scoreboard is required for commanding leads. The recorded Kalshi score can be reused for 30 seconds. Player-prop overs retain independent statistic confirmation. Independent verification may support optional allocation upgrades, but it is not a prerequisite for a new non-player entry.

Overs keep their separate allocation rules. Player-prop crossing times are persisted separately from the general ten-minute candidate window. Live games with an active crossed prop receive market-scan priority until the crossing disappears or the game ends. An exact independent player-stat match remains mandatory both before and after the ten-minute wait.

Live score/quote watchers take priority over queued catalog and weather reads without increasing the shared API rate limit. Known live milestones are processed while full discovery runs in the background; completed discovery is cached for 60 seconds across invocations. All active games are registered for live checking before event-market discovery, with near-window leaders checked in clock order. Private `execution_diagnostics` records each candidate's last gate, score, clock, possession, quote and sizing; no new rejection UI is added. `combo_exclusions` remembers exchange-rejected contract terms and is invalidated by changed terms.

The fast lead watcher polls all recently registered live games and applies the winner rule to each new score, even if the last full scan marked the game as outside its lead window. It uses every linked whole-game winner event, including late baseball games without a countdown clock. A separate fast pass takes previously identified, executable cushioned-over tickets straight to a fresh Kalshi score, current contract and orderbook, cash and net-profit checks, then the existing fill-or-kill submission gate. The prior scan identifies the ticket; it never substitutes an old quote or score for the final order checks. Markets with no resting YES ask remain visible as blocked signals rather than being treated as pending orders.

## Deployment

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

Discovery traverses the Sports and Esports milestone pages for games and tournaments started in the last seven days. It follows pagination until future-only pages, drops definitively completed older milestones from live-data polling, retains unknown and potentially active statuses, and rechecks Kalshi live data. The dashboard separately reports milestones assessed and live games checked; the latter is not the size of Kalshi's sports catalog. Supported strategies still require the exact linked, open Kalshi event and market.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

Required private Neon Function environment variables:

| Name | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | Password for the dashboard sign-in (`rjdc` username). A successful sign-in issues a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Legacy Basic credentials remain accepted for API clients without a browser challenge. |
| `KALSHI_API_KEY_ID` (or `KALSHI_API_KEY`) | Kalshi API key ID. |
| `KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY` | Kalshi private key as RSA PEM, Ed25519 PEM, or base64-encoded PKCS#8 DER. PEM accepts real newlines or escaped `\\n`. |
| `LIVE_TRADING` | `true` to allow real orders; otherwise scanner and dashboard remain read-only. |
| `EXIT_ONLY` | `true` with `LIVE_TRADING=false` to monitor held leads and submit comeback sells while blocking all new buys. |
| `MAX_YES_PRICE` | Optional price ceiling, default `1.00`. |
| `FEE_MULTIPLIER_RESERVE` | Optional affordability buffer, default twice the standard Kalshi taker fee estimate. |
| `AI_API_KEY` | Optional Vireonix AI key for the redundant order recovery monitor; the A+ protocol omits Authorization if absent. |
| `AI_BASE_URL`, `AI_MODEL` | Optional Vireonix overrides; defaults match A+ (`https://vireonix.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `auto`). |

GitHub repository secrets are not readable after creation and are not automatically transferred to a Neon Function. Existing readable GitHub variables can be securely copied during a Neon deployment; the values must not be committed. Never commit them to this public repository or expose them to the browser.

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. Filled open positions appear in the orders card; settled positions drop out of that card while Kalshi's current account balance reflects their settlement. Uncertain submissions and interrupted reservations are checked against Kalshi order history before a single idempotent recovery attempt with the same client order ID. Vireonix reviews missing submissions when configured; if it is unavailable, deterministic recovery remains active. Explicit exchange rejections remain visible in the attempt record; a rejected order is retried only after a relevant quote or available-balance change. A fill-or-kill order with no fill can be attempted again on a later scan while the signal still qualifies. Earlier attempt details are retained in `attempt_history`. Orders use a unique client ID and a database uniqueness constraint. A preflight sizes each order within available cash and its allocation, including a conservative fee estimate. The final buy gate recounts the same confirmed open positions as the filled-orders card, reserves recent in-flight slots, and checks the applicable net profit floor at the executable limit or combo quote before submission. When the cash is on a different Kalshi exchange shard, the app requests an idempotent internal transfer, records it in `funding_transfers`, and waits for actual destination cash before attempting the order. Transfers do not move funds outside the Kalshi account. Kalshi can still reject or leave an order unfilled if its quote, liquidity, fee schedule or account state changes.

## Local checks

Run `npm install && npm test && npm run build`. The app can be served locally with `npm run dev` when `DATABASE_URL` and `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` are set. The test suite covers threshold strictness, delayed data, lead requirements and bankroll sizing. Before enabling live trading, verify Kalshi credentials against the balance endpoint and confirm actual market/live data field shapes in a read-only scan.

NBA player overs also check FOX player box scores when available. A disagreement vetoes entry; missing FOX data does not override successful Kalshi and ESPN checks. Timestamped provider responses and verification decisions are stored privately in verification_records, with single-order links in order_verifications. The filled-orders sell button shows estimated net sale profit or loss at the best resting YES bid and submits a user-requested, reduce-only IOC sale, bounded by current holdings and bid depth. A changed lower price requires refreshing the quote.

NBA official, ESPN/FOX, CBS, and Yahoo requests run in parallel. For NBA markets, a matched official NBA statistic governs; if unavailable, an ESPN fixture and statistic take priority, followed by FOX when ESPN is unavailable. CBS (NBA player boxscores) and Yahoo (NBA player and whole-game scores) are last-resort alternatives when the preferred checks are unavailable; conflicting fallback readings fail verification. Other sports use ESPN first and FOX as a fallback where coverage exists. Source requests alone never replace the separate Kalshi observations. Combinations can draw on distinct eligible events with a compatible all-YES collection; a maker quote and all verification gates are still required.

Draw entries: an explicit draw/tie contract, a tied live final-period score, and a moving verified clock strictly below 50 seconds for soccer or 45 seconds for other supported sports qualify for a maximum 5% bankroll allocation. The two score reads must preserve the tie and show time decreasing; a missing ask or contradictory external score blocks entry. Rugby winner entries require at least a 20-point lead in the second half strictly under three minutes (10% raw tier), or at least 27 points (15% raw tier, reduced to 10% without control). With only Kalshi score verification, the rugby lead must still qualify after removing a seven-point score; a 27-point lead is the minimum and the allocation rises from 5% to 10% with the remaining margin and confirmed possession.
