# K — sports and weather trading

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi trading dashboard. The sports scanner reads linked live milestones, event markets and actual YES ask depth (resting NO bids). It revisits score matches when an ask appears. A score match without an executable ask is displayed but cannot fill.

- **Exceeded overs:** Every over entry requires a removable cushion on the exact selected statistic in both Kalshi and fresh independent external evidence. Soccer needs one extra goal; other game, team and period totals retain their conservative sport-specific allowance (basketball four points, football eight, baseball four, hockey one). A clock expiring cannot authorize an uncushioned over. Non-player cushioned overs require an executable YES seller and a successful fresh external request, with proof expiring after 10 seconds. Countable player overs require one removable statistic and two independent external checks: the first at least 20 seconds after the initial seller-backed observation, the second paired with a fresh Kalshi read at least 1 minute 50 seconds after that initial observation. Basketball countable props cap at 10%; other countable props at 25%, including holdings, fees and add-ons. Non-player cushions retain the 40% ceiling; externally established two- and three-unit cushions can permit 50% and 60%, preserving any larger sport-specific allowance. Cash, fees and profit gate every entry, retry and addition.
Player props take priority among executable overs early in a game and require at least 10 minutes of regulation remaining. Baseball player props close after the seventh inning; a sport without a reliable time or phase equivalent cannot authorize a player-prop entry. Team and game totals keep their separate over timing. Player prop overs in quarter-based sports require at least **$0.09 net settlement profit after reserved fees in Q1** and **$0.06 in Q2**, per new buy or add-on. Other game, team and period overs retain their **$0.15 Q1** and **$0.10 Q2** floors. Higher filled-order profit tiers still apply. Later periods and sports played in halves, innings or hockey periods retain their existing profit requirements. The same check applies to preflight, fast entries, missing-order recovery and final submission.
Variable-value YES player props (passing/rushing/receiving yards, basketball points/PRA, baseball RBI) are disabled because they have no safe fixed one-play cushion. The separate NO yardage strategy retains its existing rules. Countable YES props require the credited extra statistic in independent evidence as well as Kalshi.

- **Revised winner entries:** CFB 18+ at 7:30 or less, 15+ at 5:00 or less, or 12+ at 4:30 or less in Q4; NFL 11+ at 7:30 or less, or 8+ at 2:00 or less in Q4, without possession; NBA +16 strictly under 4:30, or +7 strictly under 1:45 in Q4; WNBA +11 strictly under 4:30 or +7 strictly under 1:45 in Q4; men’s and women’s college basketball +18 strictly under 4:30 or +14 strictly under 1:45 in the final period (men: second half; women: Q4); international basketball +10 strictly under 4:30 in Q4. An additional winner route allows NBA +7, college +14, or international +7 strictly under 1:45 in the final period, with either team in possession or possession unknown. This route requires independent external score and clock verification concurrently with the executable YES ask/depth check, and caps total entry cost including fees at 15% of bankroll. Existing higher-lead routes retain their rules. College and international basketball do not require possession; hockey 3+ goals at 6:45 or less, or 2+ goals at 3:45 or less in period 3; baseball 5+ runs at the bottom of the eighth with two outs; ninth-inning winner entries are disabled; soccer +3 goals strictly under 15:00 remaining, or +2 goals strictly under 3:30 remaining. An absent countdown uses the elapsed regulation minute; unknown stoppage time requires an explicit remaining countdown. Rugby +15 in the second half strictly under 3:00 without the former higher allocation tier; limited-over cricket 15+ runs ahead with at most nine legal balls and two wickets left in a live, unadjusted chase; golf +4 strokes in the final round with every golfer through at least 17 holes and one through exactly 17. Table tennis requires the leader at **10 points with the opponent at 5 or fewer**, in the final deciding set with sets tied; no live clock is required. Earlier 8–4, 9–5 and 10–6 entry states are disabled. Tennis requires both players tied at one set each (best of three) or two sets each (best of five), with the selected player serving on match point: 40–0, 40–15, 40–30, or the selected player’s advantage at deuce, at 5–0 through 5–4 or 6–5 games. One more point must win the match; tiebreak formats remain unsupported. The unrevised handball, lacrosse, volleyball, darts and draw rules remain. Superseded early basketball, football and baseball winner routes and last-seconds exceptions are removed. Live quotes, exact contract identity, score confirmation, mandatory fresh independent verification, cash, and net profit still gate every purchase.
- **Current-day weather (paused):** When enabled, the scanner discovers rain and temperature series across cities and checks the designated station and city's local date. Entry is available throughout the named local calendar day until the earlier of market close and its local-day cutoff. The same Kalshi contract/rules/status are checked twice at least three seconds apart; an in-period matching official station observation must establish an irreversible selected outcome. No forecast-confidence percentage is required. A forecast alone cannot confirm an observed outcome. Missing or inapplicable external station data and ranges that can still change fail closed. An NWS climate measurement day can extend into the next local day during daylight saving time, but entry still stops on the named local day. Weather's measurement window is separate from sports game clocks.
- **Combinations:** Each new confirmed single fill starts a check of currently held eligible singles. The planner visits every permitted unordered subset, from pairs through the collection's maximum size, including every alternative player/contract choice when a collection allows one market per event. A persistent cursor and durable queue resume bounded batches across scans and restarts. Twelve RFQ plans is a per-batch limit, not a lifetime coverage limit. A+B and B+A share one canonical record, as do reordered triples and larger groups, across collections and new-fill/general checks. Each exact set receives at most one automatic RFQ; failed/no-quote attempts stay recorded rather than being requested again. Existing recorded attempts are imported. Work stopped before RFQ creation resumes from its saved stage; uncertain external requests are retained without blindly repeating the POST. An immediate batch includes its newly filled leg. Pending queues retain other distinct sets for later general passes. Only actual confirmed, still-held singles reach market/RFQ creation; unfilled-signal diagnostics perform no exchange writes. Collections and all pages are discovered from Kalshi, and every selected leg must meet its contract, collection, holding, exclusion, cash, fee, profit and order-idempotency gates. An all-winner combination with independently verified legs is capped at 35% of bankroll including fees; any fallback winner or over leg keeps the combination capped at 15%. A new confirmed single may combine with still-held legs of an existing combo to form a distinct larger ticket; the prior exact leg set is never submitted again, and fallback legs retain the 15% per-combo ceiling. Existing add-on and uncertain-order reconciliation controls remain. Weather scanning is paused.


Commanding-lead entries use one internally consistent Kalshi score recorded within 30 seconds, then a current seller on the exact qualifying contract. Independent external verification is mandatory for every purchase, including higher-lead winner routes; the additional NBA +7, college +14, and international +7 under-1:45 routes requires independent verification and caps at 15%, including fees. Very late commanding leads may allocate up to 20% (NBA +20, other basketball +16 strictly under 1:45; hockey +5 strictly under 2:00; football +21 strictly under 1:30; soccer +3 after 85′). Other ordinary leads cap at 15%, and ties at 5%. An independently matched live scoreboard allows 25% for a very late commanding lead, rising to 30% at NBA +30, other basketball +25, hockey +7, football +28, or soccer +4 within their late windows. A conflicting independent score blocks the order; an unavailable source blocks every entry; it cannot authorize a lower-allocation fallback. Overs use their applicable seller-backed cushion or timed independent route above. A failed or missing seller check cannot produce an order. Existing cash, fee, profit, duplicate-order and fill-or-kill controls apply. Independent checks remain useful for optional position upgrades and diagnostics.

An independently verified lead combination must reach at least 10% and cannot exceed 35% of current bankroll, counting its existing exposure and fees on add-ons. A smaller maker quote cannot produce an order. Fallback and over combinations retain their 15% ceiling.

On later scans, a held over can receive an additional buy only after the unchanged contract again qualifies with its mandatory cushion and fresh independent verification. Existing exposure, additions and paid/reserved fees count toward the applicable 10% basketball countable-prop, 25% other player-prop, or 40% non-player ceiling. Fresh external evidence of two removable units permits a 50% non-player ceiling; three or more permit 60%, preserving larger sport-specific correction allowances. Separate upgrade and verification records retain the original fill and prevent duplicate orders after uncertain responses.

## Lead entry and allocation

College football first-half (Q2) and full-game (Q4) total No entries require a listed line at least 15 points above the combined score, an independent score and clock check from 5:00 to strictly under 6:00 remaining, and a second fresh check with executable No depth strictly under 5:00. Men’s and women’s college basketball first-half (men’s half 1; women’s Q2) and full-game (men’s half 2; women’s Q4) total No entries require a line at least 40 points above the combined score, independent preflight before 4:00, and a second fresh check with No depth strictly under 4:00. Each sport’s total No holdings share their own 15% bankroll ceiling including fees; these pools are separate from player prop No holdings.

The revised winner windows are listed above. Qualification uses the score, period, clock and exact selected outcome. Entry verification checks current executable sell depth. Existing higher-lead routes retain their 30-second score window and require fresh independent external confirmation. The additional NBA +7, college +14, and international +7 routes check external evidence concurrently with live YES depth, requires compatible final-period clocks strictly under 1:45, and expires its score and proof after 10 seconds. Uncushioned overs are blocked regardless of elapsed time; cushioned overs require fresh Kalshi and independent external evidence plus an executable seller. Independent verification may support optional allocation upgrades for commanding leads.
Three-goal or larger hockey and soccer winners need an executable buy costing at least 15% of current bankroll, including reserved fees. Their ordinary sizing ceiling is 20%; independently verified late leads retain the higher 25% and 30% tiers. Insufficient cash or YES depth defers the order.

Overs keep their separate allocation rules. The cushioned seller-backed Kalshi verification and first external check are persisted separately from the general ten-minute candidate window. Live games with an active crossed prop receive market-scan priority until the crossing disappears or the game ends. An exact independent player-stat match remains mandatory for all player overs; variable-value YES props are disabled and countable props require two external checks at 20 seconds and 1:50. Eligible unit-count cushions use the immediate seller-backed route above.
Single buys and add-ons can use all available cash. If eligible single candidates collectively need more cash than available, their estimated net settlement profit after fees takes priority; each candidate still undergoes fresh verification, quote, cash and profit checks. The combo's own allocation minimum remains in effect and does not guarantee a maker quote.

Live score/quote watchers take priority over queued catalog and weather reads without increasing the shared API rate limit. Known live milestones are processed while full discovery runs in the background; completed discovery is cached for 60 seconds across invocations. All active games are registered for live checking before event-market discovery, with near-window leaders checked in clock order. Private `execution_diagnostics` records each candidate's last gate, score, clock, possession, quote and sizing; no new rejection UI is added. `combo_exclusions` remembers exchange-rejected contract terms and is invalidated by changed terms.

The fast lead watcher polls all recently registered live games and applies the winner rule to each new score, even if the last full scan marked the game as outside its lead window. It uses every linked whole-game winner event, including late baseball games without a countdown clock. A separate fast pass revisits previously identified executable over tickets through the same persisted timing and independent-verification gates, current contract and orderbook, cash and net-profit checks, then the fill-or-kill submission gate. The prior scan identifies the ticket; it never substitutes an old quote or score for the final order checks. Markets with no resting YES ask remain visible as blocked signals rather than being treated as pending orders.

Tennis single-winner positions have no automatic profit-taking exit. Neither the 10% target nor the general half-potential-profit target can sell these positions. The dedicated tennis profit watcher is removed. Deciding-set match-point entry, independently confirmed comeback protection, and manual sells remain available. Table-tennis, over and combination profit targets retain their existing rules.

## Deployment

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

Discovery traverses only Sports milestone pages for the preceding seven days and retains only families with implemented entry rules: basketball (NBA, WNBA, men’s and women’s college), baseball, football, hockey, soccer, tennis, table tennis, golf (PGA, LPGA, European Tour), cricket, handball, rugby, lacrosse, volleyball and darts. Esports and unsupported families are excluded from fresh discovery, retained caches, live polling and fast watchers. Existing international-basketball exclusions remain. Pagination continues until future-only pages; the dashboard distinguishes catalog milestones from live games checked. Each entry still requires an exact linked open contract and its applicable rule.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

Required private Neon Function environment variables:

| Name | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | Password for the dashboard sign-in (`rjdc` username). A successful sign-in issues a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Legacy Basic credentials remain accepted for API clients without a browser challenge. |
| `KALSHI_API_KEY_ID` (or `KALSHI_API_KEY`) | Kalshi API key ID. |
| `KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY` | Kalshi private key as RSA PEM, Ed25519 PEM, or base64-encoded PKCS#8 DER. PEM accepts real newlines or escaped `\\n`. |
| `LIVE_TRADING` | `true` to allow real orders; otherwise scanner and dashboard remain read-only. |
| `EXIT_ONLY` | `true` with `LIVE_TRADING=false` to monitor held leads and submit comeback sells while blocking all new buys. |
| `MAX_YES_PRICE` | Optional price ceiling, default `1.00`. |
| `FEE_MULTIPLIER_RESERVE` | Optional affordability buffer, default twice the standard Kalshi taker fee estimate. |
| `AI_API_KEY` | Optional Vireonix AI key for the redundant order recovery monitor; the A+ protocol omits Authorization if absent. |
| `AI_BASE_URL`, `AI_MODEL` | Optional Vireonix overrides; defaults match A+ (`https://vireonix.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `auto`). |

GitHub repository secrets are not readable after creation and are not automatically transferred to a Neon Function. Existing readable GitHub variables can be securely copied during a Neon deployment; the values must not be committed. Never commit them to this public repository or expose them to the browser.

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. Filled open positions appear in the orders card; settled positions drop out of that card while Kalshi's current account balance reflects their settlement. Uncertain submissions and interrupted reservations are checked against Kalshi order history before a single idempotent recovery attempt with the same client order ID. Vireonix reviews missing submissions when configured; if it is unavailable, deterministic recovery remains active. Explicit exchange rejections remain visible in the attempt record; a rejected order is retried only after a relevant quote or available-balance change. A fill-or-kill order with no fill can be attempted again on a later scan while the signal still qualifies. Earlier attempt details are retained in `attempt_history`. Orders use a unique client ID and a database uniqueness constraint. A preflight sizes each order within available cash and its allocation, including a conservative fee estimate. The final buy gate recounts the same confirmed open positions as the filled-orders card, reserves recent in-flight slots, and checks the applicable net profit floor at the executable limit or combo quote before submission. When the cash is on a different Kalshi exchange shard, the app requests an idempotent internal transfer, records it in `funding_transfers`, and waits for actual destination cash before attempting the order. Transfers do not move funds outside the Kalshi account. Kalshi can still reject or leave an order unfilled if its quote, liquidity, fee schedule or account state changes.

## Local checks

Run `npm install && npm test && npm run build`. The app can be served locally with `npm run dev` when `DATABASE_URL` and `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` are set. The test suite covers threshold strictness, delayed data, lead requirements and bankroll sizing. Before enabling live trading, verify Kalshi credentials against the balance endpoint and confirm actual market/live data field shapes in a read-only scan.

NBA player overs also check FOX player box scores when available. A disagreement vetoes entry; missing FOX data does not override successful Kalshi and ESPN checks. Timestamped provider responses and verification decisions are stored privately in verification_records, with single-order links in order_verifications. The filled-orders sell button shows estimated net sale profit or loss at the best resting YES bid and submits a user-requested, reduce-only IOC sale, bounded by current holdings and bid depth. A changed lower price requires refreshing the quote.

ESPN is the primary independent provider. When ESPN cannot supply the exact fixture/statistic, Sportradar starts immediately, with no ESPN retry and no wait for the parallel official NBA check. An ESPN or official NBA contradiction still blocks entry. If Sportradar is also unavailable, the existing NBA/CBS/Yahoo/FOX fallback checks remain. Source requests alone never replace the separate Kalshi observations. The backend uses `SPORTRADAR_API_KEY` and `SPORTRADAR_ACCESS_LEVEL` (`trial` by default), sending the key only in the `x-api-key` header. Keys must never be committed or exposed to the browser. Sport adapters require exact opponent/date identity, live status and the selected statistic; missing coverage fails closed. Golf uses its own leaderboard adapter; table tennis uses Sportradar before its existing Sofascore check because ESPN has no mapped feed. See [provider checks and trial limits](docs/provider-comparison-20261102.md).

External fixture matching normalizes known team abbreviation variants within their own league, including Washington's WAS/WSH codes. The matchup, home/away orientation, start time, score, clock and selected player statistic remain separate checks. FOX calendar sports use its current league score-segment routes; NFL and CFB discover the provider's current season/week segment. FOX football player checks read the named passing, rushing or receiving table rather than another yardage column. Unavailable verification reports the source failure or missing fixture/statistic; a confirmed disagreement still blocks entry.

Draw entries: an explicit draw/tie contract, a tied live final-period score, and a moving verified clock strictly below 50 seconds for soccer or 45 seconds for other supported sports qualify for a maximum 5% bankroll allocation. The two score reads must preserve the tie and show time decreasing; a missing ask or contradictory external score blocks entry. Rugby winner entries require at least a 15-point lead in the second half strictly under three minutes. The direct allocation is 15%; the separate strong Kalshi-only lead check still requires that the margin qualify after removing a seven-point score.

## Dashboard reconciliation

Automatic exit fills are kept by Kalshi order ID before the per-market exit state is reused. History sums every archived partial sale and includes confirmed manual combo add-ons with their actual fills and fees. Uncertain manual orders are looked up by their client ID across paginated order history; a definitive rejection releases the pending request. The combo add-on quote uses the same 35% verified-lead or 15% fallback/over ceiling as the automatic combo engine.

History recognizes both recorded automatic exits and manual sales by exact exchange order ID, including canonical NO-side buy representations of a YES sale. Modern fixed-point execution costs and fees are supported. Completed history is reconciled again when a recorded fill or exit changes; unavailable or incomplete fills never create an estimated result. The first history page refreshes every 30 seconds while older pages remain available.

Dashboard balance, holdings and sell requests have interactive API priority within the existing shared rate limit. They bypass queued local scanner balance refreshes. Balance cache invalidation preserves the last verified snapshot and its timestamp while preventing its reuse for new orders. The displayed bankroll remains Kalshi available cash plus Kalshi portfolio value across all exchange indexes. Sell quotes refresh in batches covering every displayed holding; errors are shown as retryable quote failures rather than being silently omitted or treated as absent bids.

Independent verification preserves the provider, exact fixture, both opponents, home/away assignment, scheduled time and known sport-specific team aliases. Evidence may equal or improve the selected outcome, or independently exceed the exact over contract line; an increase in the opponent’s score alone is not favorable winner evidence. Unfamiliar aliases and unavailable independent coverage cannot authorize an entry that requires verification.

Every purchase must carry fresh independent evidence bound to the exact contract and audit record at HTTP dispatch. Missing, contradictory, stale or unsupported verification blocks the purchase, including cushioned overs and winner entries. An internal Kalshi/seller audit is a preflight only and cannot authorize a purchase.

Every filled dashboard position has an **Invest more ($)** field and **Submit** button. Submit obtains a fresh quote, then requires a second confirmation before placing a fill-or-kill buy. The confirmed dollar amount, cash, shard funds, live ask depth and fees bound the addition; the user-selected amount is independent of automatic strategy allocation caps. Fresh independent external verification is mandatory again, including every exact YES leg of a combination. Missing or contradictory evidence blocks the addition. Uncertain responses remain pending and reconcile by client order ID before another purchase is allowed.

Manual sell values use executable YES bid depth across price levels and distinguish estimated net cash proceeds from estimated profit/loss. Partial depth shows the covered quantity. Current published taker fees are estimated separately; unavailable fees never produce a fabricated net value. Quotes retain the 30-second window and are refreshed before sale confirmation. Automated exit pricing and thresholds are unchanged.

Lacrosse external verification uses ESPN’s keyless PLL, NLL, or NCAA men’s scoreboard selected from the game’s league metadata. One exact live fixture and qualifying score from the selected feed is sufficient; the three leagues are not a consensus requirement. Unknown/conflicting leagues and women’s leagues cannot borrow a men’s feed. Existing score, period, contract, freshness, and purchase gates still apply.
