# K — sports trading

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi sports trading dashboard. It discovers Kalshi Sports milestones periodically and targets a fresh live-data scan every three seconds while eligible live games are present and evaluates two strategies:

- **Verified overs:** whole-game totals compare the contract line to both teams' combined score; team totals compare it only to the contract's named team's score and verified team ID. Linked basketball player points, rebounds, assists, threes, PRA, steals, and blocks use the named player's Kalshi live stat and an exact ESPN boxscore match. ATP/WTA full-match game totals use the sum of every set's games and a matching ESPN match scoreboard. Other player and sport markets remain excluded until their individual statistic and settlement rule can be verified. Open over exposure is capped by the allocation logic and available cash.
- **Late commanding leads:** football requires a ten-point lead in the final quarter under five minutes; hockey requires three goals under seven minutes in the third period. Basketball requires ten points with under five minutes, nine points with under three minutes, or five points with under twenty seconds in the final quarter or half, each capped at 10% of bankroll. Baseball requires two runs in the ninth. Soccer requires two goals from 85:00 while regulation scoring is verified; ambiguous stoppage and extra-time feeds are excluded. Tennis requires 5–2 or better in a match-clinching set while the leader serves, with a known point score and no opponent game point. Russia Liga Pro table tennis requires a live fifth set, completed sets tied 2–2, a 10–4 or larger point lead, and an exact independent Sofascore live match; allocation is capped at 10% of bankroll. It uses set points rather than a countdown clock. Other lead allocations vary by sport and remaining time, bounded by cash, shard cash, available ask size, fees, and price. Unverified score/clock or unsupported esports and cricket formats do not qualify.

Entry profit means estimated net gain **if YES settles**, after a conservative fee reserve. Every entry needs at least $0.25 net below $100 bankroll, or $0.50 net at $100 and above; there is no separate percentage return requirement. Entries use fill-or-kill at the verified ask so a partial fill cannot fall below the profit minimum. The scanner checks actual resting NO bids for YES purchase depth before sending an order and treats an explicit insufficient-volume rejection as unfilled. Overs are never automatically sold on a comeback; only recorded lead positions can trigger that exit. Open over exposure is capped at 30% of bankroll; an individual over is also capped at 30%. A combination without an over is capped at 10% of bankroll. Unfilled candidates expire after 10 minutes.

The scanner discovers Sports milestones and linked total markets. Independent score coverage includes NBA, WNBA, NCAA men's and women's basketball, NFL, college football, NHL, MLB, soccer, and ATP/WTA match-game totals. An already-exceeded over does not require a countdown clock; it requires an independently verified matching statistic and contract. Other competitions may appear on the dashboard without independent verification, and will not enter an over merely because Kalshi reports a score. Cricket and esports have real threshold markets, but their live statistic verification is not yet integrated. Golf PGA, LPGA, and DP World Tour winner entries require a five-stroke lead in round four, every leaderboard golfer through at least 17 holes, at least one golfer still playing, and an exact ESPN match for every named golfer's total and holes completed. These entries are capped at 10%. Incomplete or cut-player boards that cannot be reconciled block entry. A contract is traded only when its own score, timing, market terms and quote can be interpreted safely. A low YES ask on a late commanding-lead market is treated as evidence of a score or clock mismatch and rejected. The dashboard counts score-rule matches separately from unfilled markets with live asks and preflight candidates. A passed over line with a zero-size ask has no seller to fill an immediate YES purchase; the scanner rechecks quotes on later cycles.

An over is only a preflight candidate when a live score first exceeds its line. Before any entry, the scanner waits at least 12 seconds, fetches the specific milestone's live data again, and requires a newer source update with the same per-team score (and the same individual player stat for supported props). Both observations must match the milestone ID and be no more than 30 seconds behind retrieval. A reversed clock, corrected score, mismatched line, or market belonging to another event blocks entry. ESPN or FOX Sports scores must match the fixture; supported player props additionally require an exact ESPN boxscore statistic on two reads separated by the Kalshi dwell. These checks run concurrently for up to 20 candidates, and execution starts as soon as each prioritized candidate's check finishes. An aged verification is repeated rather than used for an order. Public website scoreboards are not contractual data APIs and may share underlying statistics, so unavailable or mismatched coverage fails closed.

## Deployment

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

Required private Neon Function environment variables:

| Name | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | Password for the dashboard sign-in (`rjdc` username). A successful sign-in issues a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Legacy Basic credentials remain accepted for API clients without a browser challenge. |
| `KALSHI_API_KEY_ID` (or `KALSHI_API_KEY`) | Kalshi API key ID. |
| `KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY` | Kalshi private key as RSA PEM, Ed25519 PEM, or base64-encoded PKCS#8 DER. PEM accepts real newlines or escaped `\\n`. |
| `LIVE_TRADING` | `true` to allow real orders; otherwise scanner and dashboard remain read-only. |
| `EXIT_ONLY` | `true` with `LIVE_TRADING=false` to monitor held leads and submit comeback sells while blocking all new buys. |
| `MAX_YES_PRICE` | Optional price ceiling, default `1.00`. |
| `FEE_MULTIPLIER_RESERVE` | Optional affordability buffer, default twice the standard Kalshi taker fee estimate. |
| `AI_API_KEY` | Optional Vireonix AI key for the redundant order recovery monitor; the A+ protocol omits Authorization if absent. |
| `AI_BASE_URL`, `AI_MODEL` | Optional Vireonix overrides; defaults match A+ (`https://vireonix.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `auto`). |

GitHub repository secrets are not readable after creation and are not automatically transferred to a Neon Function. Existing readable GitHub variables can be securely copied during a Neon deployment; the values must not be committed. Never commit them to this public repository or expose them to the browser.

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. Filled open positions appear in the orders card; settled positions drop out of that card while Kalshi's current account balance reflects their settlement. Uncertain submissions and interrupted reservations are checked against Kalshi order history before a single idempotent recovery attempt with the same client order ID. Vireonix reviews missing submissions when configured; if it is unavailable, deterministic recovery remains active. Explicit exchange rejections remain visible in the attempt record; a rejected order is retried only after a relevant quote or available-balance change. A fill-or-kill order with no fill can be attempted again on a later scan while the signal still qualifies. Earlier attempt details are retained in `attempt_history`. Orders use a unique client ID and a database uniqueness constraint. A preflight sizes each order within available cash and its allocation, including a conservative fee estimate. When the cash is on a different Kalshi exchange shard, the app requests an idempotent internal transfer, records it in `funding_transfers`, and waits for actual destination cash before attempting the order. Transfers do not move funds outside the Kalshi account. Kalshi can still reject or leave an order unfilled if its quote, liquidity, fee schedule or account state changes.

## Local checks

Run `npm install && npm test && npm run build`. The app can be served locally with `npm run dev` when `DATABASE_URL` and `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` are set. The test suite covers threshold strictness, delayed data, lead requirements and bankroll sizing. Before enabling live trading, verify Kalshi credentials against the balance endpoint and confirm actual market/live data field shapes in a read-only scan.

NBA player overs also check FOX player box scores when available. A disagreement vetoes entry; missing FOX data does not override successful Kalshi and ESPN checks. Timestamped provider responses and verification decisions are stored privately in verification_records, with single-order links in order_verifications. The filled-orders sell button shows the best resting YES bid and submits a user-requested, reduce-only IOC sale, bounded by current holdings and bid depth. A changed lower price requires refreshing the quote.

NBA official, ESPN/FOX, CBS, and Yahoo requests run in parallel. For NBA markets, a matched official NBA statistic governs; if unavailable, an ESPN fixture and statistic take priority, followed by FOX when ESPN is unavailable. CBS (NBA player boxscores) and Yahoo (NBA player and whole-game scores) are last-resort alternatives when the preferred checks are unavailable; conflicting fallback readings fail verification. Other sports use ESPN first and FOX as a fallback where coverage exists. Source requests alone never replace the separate Kalshi observations. Combinations can draw on qualifying unfilled candidates, including same-game markets when the Kalshi collection permits them; a maker quote and all verification gates are still required.
