# K — sports and weather trading

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi trading dashboard. The sports scanner reads linked live milestones, event markets and actual YES ask depth (resting NO bids). It revisits score matches when an ask appears. A score match without an executable ask is displayed but cannot fill.

- **Exceeded overs:** All sports overs (game, team, period and player totals) require a fresh crossed Kalshi score, exact contract and executable YES seller. When removing a conservative full scoring play or player-stat allowance still leaves the selected total strictly over its line, the cushioned over can enter immediately without an external check or dwell and retains its 40% allocation tier. Otherwise the seller-backed Kalshi verification starts a durable per-contract clock: the first independent external request starts at least **20 seconds** later, and the second launches with a fresh Kalshi request at least **3 minutes** after the initial verification (**4 minutes** for variable-value player props). Both independent checks must agree on the exact selected statistic or segment; missing coverage or a contradiction blocks entry. The final paired reads expire after 10 seconds. A changed contract, lost crossing or gap over 90 seconds resets the uncushioned clock. Timed, independently verified uncushioned overs may allocate up to 20%. Cash, fees and net profit gate every entry.
Player props take priority among executable overs early in a game and require at least 10 minutes of regulation remaining. Baseball player props close after the seventh inning; a sport without a reliable time or phase equivalent cannot authorize a player-prop entry. Team and game totals keep their separate over timing. All crossed overs (player, game, team and period totals) in quarter-based sports require at least **$0.15 net settlement profit after reserved fees in Q1** and **$0.10 in Q2**, per new buy or add-on. Higher filled-order profit tiers still apply. Later periods and sports played in halves, innings or hockey periods retain their existing profit requirements. The same check applies to preflight, fast entries, missing-order recovery and final submission.
Variable-value player props (passing/rushing/receiving yards, basketball points/PRA, baseball RBI) require the second independent external and Kalshi checks at least **4 minutes** after the initial Kalshi verification and cap at **15%**. They never use the seller-only cushion. Unit-count props (steals, blocks, rebounds and similar counts) keep their existing rules. No per-play cushion is enabled; any later design must exclude under-review and nullified plays.

- **Revised winner entries:** CFB 18+ at 7:30 or less, 15+ at 5:00 or less, or 12+ at 4:30 or less in Q4; NFL 11+ at 7:30 or less, or 8+ at 2:00 or less in Q4, without possession; NBA +18 strictly under 7:30, +12 strictly under 6:30, or +9 strictly under 1:45 in Q4; WNBA +11 strictly under 6:00 or +10 strictly under 1:45 in Q4; men’s and women’s college basketball +16 strictly under 5:00 or +10 strictly under 4:30 in the final period (men: second half; women: Q4); international basketball uses those same two lead/time thresholds in Q4. A +10 lead is eligible strictly under 1:45 across these categories; NBA requires +9. College and international basketball do not require possession; hockey 3+ goals at 6:45 or less, or 2+ goals at 3:45 or less in period 3; baseball 5+ runs at the bottom of the eighth with two outs; ninth-inning winner entries are disabled; soccer +3 goals strictly under 20:00 remaining, or +2 goals strictly under 3:30 remaining. An absent countdown uses the elapsed regulation minute; unknown stoppage time requires an explicit remaining countdown. Rugby +15 in the second half strictly under 3:00 without the former higher allocation tier; limited-over cricket 15+ runs ahead with at most nine legal balls and two wickets left in a live, unadjusted chase; golf +4 strokes in the final round with every golfer through at least 17 holes and one through exactly 17. Tennis and the unrevised handball, lacrosse, volleyball, table-tennis, darts and draw rules remain. Superseded early basketball, football and baseball winner routes and last-seconds exceptions are removed. Live quotes, exact contract identity, score confirmation, independent verification or supported conservative fallback, cash, and net profit still gate every purchase.
- **Current-day weather (paused):** When enabled, the scanner discovers rain and temperature series across cities and checks the designated station and city's local date. Entry is available throughout the named local calendar day until the earlier of market close and its local-day cutoff. The same Kalshi contract/rules/status are checked twice at least three seconds apart; an in-period matching official station observation must establish an irreversible selected outcome. No forecast-confidence percentage is required. A forecast alone cannot confirm an observed outcome. Missing or inapplicable external station data and ranges that can still change fail closed. An NWS climate measurement day can extend into the next local day during daylight saving time, but entry still stops on the named local day. Weather's measurement window is separate from sports game clocks.
- **Combinations:** Each new confirmed single fill starts a check of currently held eligible singles. The planner visits every permitted unordered subset, from pairs through the collection's maximum size, including every alternative player/contract choice when a collection allows one market per event. A persistent cursor and durable queue resume bounded batches across scans and restarts. Twelve RFQ plans is a per-batch limit, not a lifetime coverage limit. A+B and B+A share one canonical record, as do reordered triples and larger groups, across collections and new-fill/general checks. Each exact set receives at most one automatic RFQ; failed/no-quote attempts stay recorded rather than being requested again. Existing recorded attempts are imported. Work stopped before RFQ creation resumes from its saved stage; uncertain external requests are retained without blindly repeating the POST. An immediate batch includes its newly filled leg. Pending queues retain other distinct sets for later general passes. Only actual confirmed, still-held singles reach market/RFQ creation; unfilled-signal diagnostics perform no exchange writes. Collections and all pages are discovered from Kalshi, and every selected leg must meet its contract, collection, holding, exclusion, cash, fee, profit and order-idempotency gates. An all-winner combination with independently verified legs is capped at 35% of bankroll including fees; any fallback winner or over leg keeps the combination capped at 15%. A new confirmed single may combine with still-held legs of an existing combo to form a distinct larger ticket; the prior exact leg set is never submitted again, and fallback legs retain the 15% per-combo ceiling. Existing add-on and uncertain-order reconciliation controls remain. Weather scanning is paused.


Commanding-lead entries use one internally consistent Kalshi score recorded within 30 seconds, then a current seller on the exact qualifying contract. External scoreboards are optional for winner entries. Very late commanding leads may allocate up to 20% (NBA +20, other basketball +16 strictly under 1:45; hockey +5 strictly under 2:00; football +21 strictly under 1:30; soccer +3 after 85′). Other Kalshi-only leads cap at 15%, and ties at 5%. An independently matched live scoreboard allows 25% for a very late commanding lead, rising to 30% at NBA +30, other basketball +25, hockey +7, football +28, or soccer +4 within their late windows. A conflicting independent score blocks the order; an unavailable source leaves the Kalshi-only cap in place. Overs use their applicable seller-backed cushion or timed independent route above. A failed or missing seller check cannot produce an order. Existing cash, fee, profit, duplicate-order and fill-or-kill controls apply. Independent checks remain useful for optional position upgrades and diagnostics.

An independently verified lead combination must reach at least 10% and cannot exceed 35% of current bankroll, counting its existing exposure and fees on add-ons. A smaller maker quote cannot produce an order. Fallback and over combinations retain their 15% ceiling.

On later scans, a held over can be considered for an additional buy only after the current unchanged contract qualifies through its applicable route: immediate seller-backed cushion or timed independent verification. When confirmed through that route, it may receive one additional fill-or-kill buy on the same ticker, limited by current position exposure plus paid fees so the combined single never exceeds 20% of current bankroll including fees, or 40% when a conservative scoring play or player-stat allowance can be removed and the over remains crossed. The original fill remains intact. A separate upgrade order and verification record preserve its history and prevent an uncertain response from creating a second order. A later independent check can also make a held fallback lead eligible for the ordinary combination schedule; only a qualifying very late commanding lead can use the 20% single-order tier.

## Lead entry and allocation

The revised winner windows are listed above. Qualification uses the score, period, clock and exact selected outcome. Entry verification checks current executable sell depth; no external scoreboard is required for commanding leads. The recorded Kalshi score can be reused for 30 seconds. Uncushioned overs require the timed independent-verification sequence; cushioned overs use a fresh Kalshi score and executable seller. Independent verification may support optional allocation upgrades for commanding leads.
Three-goal or larger hockey and soccer winners need an executable buy costing at least 15% of current bankroll, including reserved fees. Their ordinary sizing ceiling is 20%; independently verified late leads retain the higher 25% and 30% tiers. Insufficient cash or YES depth defers the order.

Overs keep their separate allocation rules. The uncushioned seller-backed Kalshi verification and first external check are persisted separately from the general ten-minute candidate window. Live games with an active crossed prop receive market-scan priority until the crossing disappears or the game ends. An exact independent player-stat match remains mandatory for uncushioned player overs; variable-value props wait 4:00 and other uncushioned props wait 3:00. Eligible unit-count cushions use the immediate seller-backed route above.
Single buys and add-ons leave at least $7 of available cash for separate combinations. If eligible single candidates collectively need more cash than remains after that reserve, their estimated net settlement profit after fees takes priority; each candidate still undergoes fresh verification, quote, cash and profit checks. The $7 floor does not replace the combo's own allocation minimum or guarantee a maker quote.

Live score/quote watchers take priority over queued catalog and weather reads without increasing the shared API rate limit. Known live milestones are processed while full discovery runs in the background; completed discovery is cached for 60 seconds across invocations. All active games are registered for live checking before event-market discovery, with near-window leaders checked in clock order. Private `execution_diagnostics` records each candidate's last gate, score, clock, possession, quote and sizing; no new rejection UI is added. `combo_exclusions` remembers exchange-rejected contract terms and is invalidated by changed terms.

The fast lead watcher polls all recently registered live games and applies the winner rule to each new score, even if the last full scan marked the game as outside its lead window. It uses every linked whole-game winner event, including late baseball games without a countdown clock. A separate fast pass revisits previously identified executable over tickets through the same persisted timing and independent-verification gates, current contract and orderbook, cash and net-profit checks, then the fill-or-kill submission gate. The prior scan identifies the ticket; it never substitutes an old quote or score for the final order checks. Markets with no resting YES ask remain visible as blocked signals rather than being treated as pending orders.

## Deployment

An optional [supplemental public-data helper](workers/README.md) can assist the
existing Neon host with catalogue discovery. It does not replace Neon, run an
alternate scanner, access the database directly, or authorize trades. Neon keeps
the dashboard, records, verification, sizing and execution. An absent, expired,
invalid or unavailable helper leaves the original direct discovery path in use.

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

Discovery traverses the Sports and Esports milestone pages for games and tournaments started in the last seven days. It follows pagination until future-only pages, drops definitively completed older milestones from live-data polling, retains unknown and potentially active statuses, and rechecks Kalshi live data. The dashboard separately reports milestones assessed and live games checked; the latter is not the size of Kalshi's sports catalog. Supported strategies still require the exact linked, open Kalshi event and market.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

Required private Neon Function environment variables:

| Name | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | Password for the dashboard sign-in (`rjdc` username). A successful sign-in issues a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Legacy Basic credentials remain accepted for API clients without a browser challenge. |
| `KALSHI_API_KEY_ID` (or `KALSHI_API_KEY`) | Kalshi API key ID. |
| `KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY` | Kalshi private key as RSA PEM, Ed25519 PEM, or base64-encoded PKCS#8 DER. PEM accepts real newlines or escaped `\\n`. |
| `LIVE_TRADING` | `true` to allow real orders; otherwise scanner and dashboard remain read-only. |
| `EXIT_ONLY` | `true` with `LIVE_TRADING=false` to monitor held leads and submit comeback sells while blocking all new buys. |
| `MAX_YES_PRICE` | Optional price ceiling, default `1.00`. |
| `FEE_MULTIPLIER_RESERVE` | Optional affordability buffer, default twice the standard Kalshi taker fee estimate. |
| `AI_API_KEY` | Optional Vireonix AI key for the redundant order recovery monitor; the A+ protocol omits Authorization if absent. |
| `AI_BASE_URL`, `AI_MODEL` | Optional Vireonix overrides; defaults match A+ (`https://vireonix.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `auto`). |

GitHub repository secrets are not readable after creation and are not automatically transferred to a Neon Function. Existing readable GitHub variables can be securely copied during a Neon deployment; the values must not be committed. Never commit them to this public repository or expose them to the browser.

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. Filled open positions appear in the orders card; settled positions drop out of that card while Kalshi's current account balance reflects their settlement. Uncertain submissions and interrupted reservations are checked against Kalshi order history before a single idempotent recovery attempt with the same client order ID. Vireonix reviews missing submissions when configured; if it is unavailable, deterministic recovery remains active. Explicit exchange rejections remain visible in the attempt record; a rejected order is retried only after a relevant quote or available-balance change. A fill-or-kill order with no fill can be attempted again on a later scan while the signal still qualifies. Earlier attempt details are retained in `attempt_history`. Orders use a unique client ID and a database uniqueness constraint. A preflight sizes each order within available cash and its allocation, including a conservative fee estimate. The final buy gate recounts the same confirmed open positions as the filled-orders card, reserves recent in-flight slots, and checks the applicable net profit floor at the executable limit or combo quote before submission. When the cash is on a different Kalshi exchange shard, the app requests an idempotent internal transfer, records it in `funding_transfers`, and waits for actual destination cash before attempting the order. Transfers do not move funds outside the Kalshi account. Kalshi can still reject or leave an order unfilled if its quote, liquidity, fee schedule or account state changes.

## Local checks

Run `npm install && npm test && npm run build`. The app can be served locally with `npm run dev` when `DATABASE_URL` and `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` are set. The test suite covers threshold strictness, delayed data, lead requirements and bankroll sizing. Before enabling live trading, verify Kalshi credentials against the balance endpoint and confirm actual market/live data field shapes in a read-only scan.

NBA player overs also check FOX player box scores when available. A disagreement vetoes entry; missing FOX data does not override successful Kalshi and ESPN checks. Timestamped provider responses and verification decisions are stored privately in verification_records, with single-order links in order_verifications. The filled-orders sell button shows estimated net sale profit or loss at the best resting YES bid and submits a user-requested, reduce-only IOC sale, bounded by current holdings and bid depth. A changed lower price requires refreshing the quote.

NBA official, ESPN/FOX, CBS, and Yahoo requests run in parallel. For NBA markets, a matched official NBA statistic governs; if unavailable, an ESPN fixture and statistic take priority, followed by FOX when ESPN is unavailable. CBS (NBA player boxscores) and Yahoo (NBA player and whole-game scores) are last-resort alternatives when the preferred checks are unavailable; conflicting fallback readings fail verification. Other sports use ESPN first and FOX as a fallback where coverage exists. Source requests alone never replace the separate Kalshi observations. Combinations can draw on distinct eligible events with a compatible all-YES collection; a maker quote and all verification gates are still required.

Draw entries: an explicit draw/tie contract, a tied live final-period score, and a moving verified clock strictly below 50 seconds for soccer or 45 seconds for other supported sports qualify for a maximum 5% bankroll allocation. The two score reads must preserve the tie and show time decreasing; a missing ask or contradictory external score blocks entry. Rugby winner entries require at least a 15-point lead in the second half strictly under three minutes. The direct allocation is 15%; the separate strong Kalshi-only lead check still requires that the margin qualify after removing a seven-point score.

## Dashboard reconciliation

Automatic exit fills are kept by Kalshi order ID before the per-market exit state is reused. History sums every archived partial sale and includes confirmed manual combo add-ons with their actual fills and fees. Uncertain manual orders are looked up by their client ID across paginated order history; a definitive rejection releases the pending request. The combo add-on quote uses the same 35% verified-lead or 15% fallback/over ceiling as the automatic combo engine.

History recognizes both recorded automatic exits and manual sales by exact exchange order ID, including canonical NO-side buy representations of a YES sale. Modern fixed-point execution costs and fees are supported. Completed history is reconciled again when a recorded fill or exit changes; unavailable or incomplete fills never create an estimated result. The first history page refreshes every 30 seconds while older pages remain available.

Dashboard balance, holdings and sell requests have interactive API priority within the existing shared rate limit. They bypass queued local scanner balance refreshes. Balance cache invalidation preserves the last verified snapshot and its timestamp while preventing its reuse for new orders. The displayed bankroll remains Kalshi available cash plus Kalshi portfolio value across all exchange indexes. Sell quotes refresh in batches covering every displayed holding; errors are shown as retryable quote failures rather than being silently omitted or treated as absent bids.
