# K — sports and weather trading

K is a Neon hosted Kalshi trading dashboard. The sports scanner reads linked live milestones, event markets and actual YES ask depth (resting NO bids). It revisits score matches when an ask appears. A score match without an executable ask is displayed but cannot fill.

- **Exceeded overs:** Confirm an exact game, team, segment or supported player threshold with two fresh Kalshi live-data reads. A matching external check accompanies the first read, then the second follows immediately; only a supported Kalshi-only fallback waits ten seconds. Player props require an exact independent player-stat check. When independent checks are unavailable, supported non-player overs can qualify only if they remain above the line after removing one conservative sport-specific scoring play, at 5%–15% per game including fees; a supplied update timestamp must advance between reads. An independent contradiction blocks entry. A matched ESPN soccer whole-game over also requires recorded goal events to clear its line; without matching external coverage, the two Kalshi reads must still clear the line after removing one goal. The exact crossed statistic and contract must remain valid. Overs have no late-game clock condition and are not automatically sold for a comeback.
- **Commanding leads:** Basketball needs +5 in the final 60 seconds; NFL +10 in the final seven minutes; CFB +11 strictly under 4:30 in Q4; hockey +3 in the final 6:30; baseball +2 in the ninth; soccer +3 near the end, +2 with under five minutes on a final-whistle countdown, or +1 with under 30 seconds on that countdown. Tennis needs a match-clinching 5–4 or 6–5 deciding-set lead while serving. Handball (+5/+8 in three minutes), rugby (+15/+22 in five minutes), lacrosse (+4/+7 in three minutes), volleyball (+3/+5 at 12+ in the deciding set), and table tennis (+4/+6 at 8+ in the deciding game) have sport-specific rules. Darts 501 requires explicit checkout, remaining-point, double, next-throw and match-leg data. Two Kalshi reads are taken without a timed wait after one matching external check. When independent checks are unavailable and two Kalshi reads reconfirm the same qualifying game state, the selected winner must still qualify after removing one conservative sport-specific score from its lead; the fallback scales from 5% to 15% with the remaining margin and confirmed leader possession. Late soccer commanding leads must remain at least three goals ahead after removing one goal. A supplied update timestamp must advance and an independent contradiction blocks entry. Baseball uses inning state without a countdown clock. Cricket winner entries require a verified limited-over final innings, at least a 50-run lead, no more than 12 balls and three wickets left for the chase, a YES ask of at least 90¢, and a matching team contract; absent ball or wicket data blocks entry. Test-match winner entries remain suspended.
- **Current-day weather:** The scanner discovers rain and temperature series across cities and checks the designated station and city's local date. Entry is available throughout the named local calendar day until the earlier of market close and its local-day cutoff. The same Kalshi contract/rules/status are checked twice at least three seconds apart; an in-period matching official station observation must establish an irreversible selected outcome. No forecast-confidence percentage is required. A forecast alone cannot confirm an observed outcome. Missing or inapplicable external station data and ranges that can still change fail closed. An NWS climate measurement day can extend into the next local day during daylight saving time, but entry still stops on the named local day. Weather's measurement window is separate from sports game clocks.
- **Combinations:** Each new confirmed single fill immediately starts a fresh check against current account positions and the confirmed-fill ledger, including fills from the fast watcher, recovered orders, weather singles, and single-position upgrades. The checker considers every eligible held single, from pairs up to the collection's permitted size, when a Kalshi all-YES collection supports the exact legs and a maker returns an executable RFQ quote. A combination containing an unverified Kalshi-only leg is capped at 15% of bankroll including fees; when every leg has independent verification, the allocation ceiling is 50%. An unverified fallback leg may appear in at most one active or filled combination. A later independent verification can add to an existing combination position, with current position exposure and fees limiting total allocation to 50%, without selling its original fill. Immediate plans must include the newly filled leg. Combo checking runs outside the live single-order path; its work is awaited before the invocation ends. A newer fill interrupts an older unaccepted RFQ wait and starts a fresh check, while duplicate fill observations share the existing claim. Existing combo fills do not recursively create new combo checks. Rejected combinations and exact exchange errors are retained privately for the scanner; redundant thresholds and previously exchange-decided legs are excluded before new RFQs. Weather trades are independent orders.

Verified over singles can use up to 30% of current bankroll; verified lead singles use the sport-specific sizing rules below. Kalshi-only fallback singles use at most 15% and one open fallback position per game, only when the crossed threshold survives a one-score deduction. Overs and leads require positive projected settlement profit after entry fees below 10 open filled orders, at least $0.15 from 10, $0.35 from 15, and $0.50 from 20. Existing higher minimums still apply. All entries need actual resting liquidity. Entries are fill-or-kill; uncertain orders are reconciled by client order ID. A shard transfer is requested only when needed and must complete before an order is submitted. The dashboard refreshes possession markers next to live scores and shows estimated net profit or loss for the shares that can currently be sold at the bid. Non-soccer commanding-lead singles and combinations target a full-position sale when net sale profit after fees reaches at least 50% of the possible net settlement profit; soccer commanding leads and combinations containing a soccer leg do not use this profit target. Other holdings retain their 1.5-times-cost target. Independently confirmed comeback exits remain available for single leads. If an officially credited statistic is corrected below a held over line in any supported sport, a separate roughly two-second live-data watcher attempts to sell the affected single or combination at an executable bid without waiting for the full scan or a profit target.

On later scans, a held Kalshi-only over is independently checked against the same live contract and two Kalshi observations. When independently confirmed, it may receive one additional fill-or-kill buy on the same ticker, limited by current position exposure plus paid fees so the combined single never exceeds 30% of current bankroll including fees. The original fill remains intact. A separate upgrade order and verification record preserve its history and prevent an uncertain response from creating a second order. A later independent check can also make a held fallback lead eligible for the ordinary combination schedule; it does not raise that lead's single-order 15% fallback limit.

## Lead entry and allocation

College football (CFB) commanding-lead entry requires Q4 strictly below 4:30 and a lead of at least 11 points, regardless of possession. Both verification reads must meet that window and margin. The former seven-point final-seconds exception and the earlier 28+ Q3 / 21+ Q4 CFB tier are disabled. Zero time, wrong period, mismatched contracts, conflicting scores, stale data, absent executable liquidity, fees and profit gates still block entry. NFL ordinary entry rules remain unchanged.

CFB ordinary lead allocations, before the possession bonus:

| Time remaining in Q4 | Lead 11–13 | Lead 14–20 | Lead 21+ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3:00 to under 4:30 | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| 1:30 to under 3:00 | 10% | 20% | 25% |
| Under 1:30 | 15% | 25% | 35% |

Leader possession adds five percentage points, capped at 35% for CFB. Thus a barely qualifying entry does not receive the maximum. The 35% tier requires a lead of at least 21 points with strictly under 90 seconds and independent verification. These are explicit allocation rules, not claims of a calibrated win probability.

The earlier NFL football tier keeps the 28+ lead with under 1:00 in Q3, or 21+ in Q4, and now uses a **10% maximum**. It starts at 5%, adds two points of bankroll allocation when the margin exceeds that segment's minimum by at least seven points, adds one point with under 7:30 in Q4, and adds two points for leader possession. Ordinary late-entry rules take precedence when they qualify.

Across supported sports, independently verified lead allocations receive a five-percentage-point control bonus when both observations confirm leader possession, serve, or next turn. Missing control data earns no bonus; tied games, overs and weather do not receive a leader bonus. CFB and early-football sizing already include their possession adjustments. Existing one-goal soccer possession tiers are retained without double-counting; other newly boosted tiers stop at 35%, preserving the pre-existing 40% soccer ceiling. **Kalshi-only lead fallback needs a margin that still qualifies after removing one conservative sport-specific score; allocation rises from 5% to 15% including fees with the remaining lead and confirmed leader possession.** Unsupported scoring shapes, player statistics, and an independent contradiction do not qualify.

Live score/quote watchers take priority over queued catalog and weather reads without increasing the shared API rate limit. Known live milestones are processed while full discovery runs in the background; completed discovery is cached for 60 seconds across invocations. All active games are registered for live checking before event-market discovery, with near-window leaders checked in clock order. Private `execution_diagnostics` records each candidate's last gate, score, clock, possession, quote and sizing; no new rejection UI is added. `combo_exclusions` remembers exchange-rejected contract terms and is invalidated by changed terms.

## Deployment

Neon project: `floral-wave-57267243`; branch: `br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7`; function slug: `k`. Neon injects `DATABASE_URL` from that branch. Apply [schema.sql](schema.sql) once, bundle the function with `npm run build`, zip `dist/index.mjs` at the archive root, then deploy the zip to Neon Functions. The scheduled trigger `k-sports-scan` invokes `/tick` every minute. An invocation runs repeated scans at a three-second target cadence while relevant live games are active; it stops when none are present. Slow API responses can extend an individual cycle beyond three seconds. Idle discovery can therefore lag by up to one minute. Neon free limits do not support uninterrupted three-second scanning around the clock.

Discovery traverses the Sports and Esports milestone pages for games and tournaments started in the last seven days. It follows pagination until future-only pages, drops definitively completed older milestones from live-data polling, retains unknown and potentially active statuses, and rechecks Kalshi live data. The dashboard separately reports milestones assessed and live games checked; the latter is not the size of Kalshi's sports catalog. Supported strategies still require the exact linked, open Kalshi event and market.

The dashboard includes an Android install button, opt-in push notifications, and an opt-in synthesized cha-ching sound. Settlement alerts are generated only from Kalshi settlement records where the YES outcome has positive net revenue after recorded cost and fees. Push delivery uses the browser's Push API and the system notification sound when the app is closed; a custom cha-ching sound requires an open, sound-enabled app and can be paused by mobile background limits. The PWA service worker never caches account or trade responses. Push subscriptions and retryable deliveries are stored in Neon, while the VAPID identity is derived from the existing Kalshi private key (rotating that key requires resubscribing).

Required private Neon Function environment variables:

| Name | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` | Password for the dashboard sign-in (`rjdc` username). A successful sign-in issues a seven-day, secure, HTTP-only session cookie. Legacy Basic credentials remain accepted for API clients without a browser challenge. |
| `KALSHI_API_KEY_ID` (or `KALSHI_API_KEY`) | Kalshi API key ID. |
| `KALSHI_PRIVATE_KEY` | Kalshi private key as RSA PEM, Ed25519 PEM, or base64-encoded PKCS#8 DER. PEM accepts real newlines or escaped `\\n`. |
| `LIVE_TRADING` | `true` to allow real orders; otherwise scanner and dashboard remain read-only. |
| `EXIT_ONLY` | `true` with `LIVE_TRADING=false` to monitor held leads and submit comeback sells while blocking all new buys. |
| `MAX_YES_PRICE` | Optional price ceiling, default `1.00`. |
| `FEE_MULTIPLIER_RESERVE` | Optional affordability buffer, default twice the standard Kalshi taker fee estimate. |
| `AI_API_KEY` | Optional Vireonix AI key for the redundant order recovery monitor; the A+ protocol omits Authorization if absent. |
| `AI_BASE_URL`, `AI_MODEL` | Optional Vireonix overrides; defaults match A+ (`https://vireonix.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `auto`). |

GitHub repository secrets are not readable after creation and are not automatically transferred to a Neon Function. Existing readable GitHub variables can be securely copied during a Neon deployment; the values must not be committed. Never commit them to this public repository or expose them to the browser.

The dashboard is available at `https://br-tiny-hill-b5aiqeg7-k.compute.c-7.us-east-2.aws.neon.tech/`. Its bankroll card shows Kalshi available cash plus portfolio value, with both components shown separately. The balance and positions are fetched independently so a positions failure cannot blank a valid balance; neither is estimated from the local ledger. Filled open positions appear in the orders card; settled positions drop out of that card while Kalshi's current account balance reflects their settlement. Uncertain submissions and interrupted reservations are checked against Kalshi order history before a single idempotent recovery attempt with the same client order ID. Vireonix reviews missing submissions when configured; if it is unavailable, deterministic recovery remains active. Explicit exchange rejections remain visible in the attempt record; a rejected order is retried only after a relevant quote or available-balance change. A fill-or-kill order with no fill can be attempted again on a later scan while the signal still qualifies. Earlier attempt details are retained in `attempt_history`. Orders use a unique client ID and a database uniqueness constraint. A preflight sizes each order within available cash and its allocation, including a conservative fee estimate. The final buy gate recounts the same confirmed open positions as the filled-orders card, reserves recent in-flight slots, and checks the applicable net profit floor at the executable limit or combo quote before submission. When the cash is on a different Kalshi exchange shard, the app requests an idempotent internal transfer, records it in `funding_transfers`, and waits for actual destination cash before attempting the order. Transfers do not move funds outside the Kalshi account. Kalshi can still reject or leave an order unfilled if its quote, liquidity, fee schedule or account state changes.

## Local checks

Run `npm install && npm test && npm run build`. The app can be served locally with `npm run dev` when `DATABASE_URL` and `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD` are set. The test suite covers threshold strictness, delayed data, lead requirements and bankroll sizing. Before enabling live trading, verify Kalshi credentials against the balance endpoint and confirm actual market/live data field shapes in a read-only scan.

NBA player overs also check FOX player box scores when available. A disagreement vetoes entry; missing FOX data does not override successful Kalshi and ESPN checks. Timestamped provider responses and verification decisions are stored privately in verification_records, with single-order links in order_verifications. The filled-orders sell button shows estimated net sale profit or loss at the best resting YES bid and submits a user-requested, reduce-only IOC sale, bounded by current holdings and bid depth. A changed lower price requires refreshing the quote.

NBA official, ESPN/FOX, CBS, and Yahoo requests run in parallel. For NBA markets, a matched official NBA statistic governs; if unavailable, an ESPN fixture and statistic take priority, followed by FOX when ESPN is unavailable. CBS (NBA player boxscores) and Yahoo (NBA player and whole-game scores) are last-resort alternatives when the preferred checks are unavailable; conflicting fallback readings fail verification. Other sports use ESPN first and FOX as a fallback where coverage exists. Source requests alone never replace the separate Kalshi observations. Combinations can draw on distinct eligible events with a compatible all-YES collection; a maker quote and all verification gates are still required.

### Late-game value research (no orders)

The dashboard now lists final-five-minute moneyline research candidates in soccer, basketball, football and hockey: leaders offered at 85¢ or higher and trailing teams offered below 50¢. Both teams are inspected against exact event and team identities. The scanner records point-in-time price, size, game clock, score and feed timestamps in `value_scan_observations`; it does not submit orders from these candidates. An ask or the exchange's displayed percentage is not an independent probability estimate.

Clock-free sports are intentionally excluded from the five-minute definition until a tested, sport-specific endgame equivalent is available. Baseball, tennis, cricket, golf, combat, racing, and esports need their own state models; an arbitrary inning, set, over, or round is not assumed to equal five minutes. Existing production strategies are unchanged.

A future value-order path must compare Kalshi's executable ask with an independent sport-specific forecast using historical team statistics and the live sport state. The model win chance must be strictly above 90%, its conservative probability lower bound must exceed the fillable ask plus applicable fees, and matching model validation must show a positive out-of-sample net-return lower bound from point-in-time executable quotes. `validatedValueDecision` rejects missing or failed evidence and reports the model-versus-Kalshi gap. An ask is the purchase price and a rough quote-implied chance, not proof of a calibrated true probability. The research observations are input data, not a trained probability model or a validated profitable strategy. An underdog can be observed but cannot auto-enter under the universal 90% model-chance floor unless the model estimates it above 90% despite the exchange quote; that disagreement would require an especially careful identity and feed review.

Draw entries: an explicit draw/tie contract, a tied live final-period score, and a moving verified clock strictly below 45 seconds qualify for a maximum 5% bankroll allocation. The two score reads must preserve the tie and show time decreasing; a missing ask or contradictory external score blocks entry. Rugby winner entries require at least a 15-point lead in the second half at five minutes or less (10% tier), or at least 22 points (15% tier). With only Kalshi score verification, the rugby lead must still qualify after removing a seven-point score; a 22-point lead is the minimum and the allocation rises from 5% to 15% with the remaining margin and confirmed possession.
